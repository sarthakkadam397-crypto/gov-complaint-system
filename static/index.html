Citizen Journey

Transparent 4-Step Resolution Process

Every complaint filed in the system is auditable, assigned to authorized nodal officers, and tracked until resolution.

1

Register Online

Fill out the simple form, specify the department, upload photos or documents, and submit instantly.

2

Receive Unique ID

Instantly receive an official 14-digit Complaint ID (e.g. GOV-2026-XXXXX) with printable acknowledgement slip.

3

Department Action

The grievance is triaged and forwarded to the nodal officer for immediate on-ground investigation.

4

Resolution & Feedback

Get notified when the issue is resolved, view final officer closure remarks, and submit satisfaction rating.
